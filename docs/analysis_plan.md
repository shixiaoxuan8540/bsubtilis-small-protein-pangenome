# Analysis decisions (draft)

- Annotated baseline: protein length <=100 aa; preserve longer homologs at family level and flag length variation.
- 168 and NCIB 3610 count toward the 20-genome core/pangenome but not niche enrichment.
- Require original isolation metadata; probiotic use does not establish gut origin.
- Resolve species identity with ANI and core-genome phylogeny.
- Count ortholog families, not synonyms or protein names.
- Distinguish annotated, homology-rescued, pseudogene/fragment, and confidently absent states.
- Evaluate plasmid and chromosomal loci separately.
- Three genomes per niche support descriptive, hypothesis-generating comparisons, not robust ecological causality.
- Core: 20/20; soft core: >=19/20; niche core: all isolates in niche; niche union: at least one isolate in niche.
